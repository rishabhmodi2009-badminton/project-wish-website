Who we support

Make-A-Wish Singapore

For children under 18

Grants wishes to children fighting critical illnesses. A wish gives a child something to look forward to during treatment, and something other than illness to talk about at home.

makeawish.org.sg →

Ambulance Wish Singapore

For adults at the end of life

Fulfils one final wish for terminally ill adults: a trip to the sea, a family wedding, a last meal somewhere that mattered. Its line is “Fulfilling Wishes, Celebrating Lives.” We've worked with them since before the club existed.

02

What we actually do

  1. Fundraise

    AMBY blind bag sales at school, fundraising events in the community, and the first social media fundraiser SAS had ever run.

  2. Tell the stories

    Members make short videos for Ambulance Wish's official pages and write awareness pieces for its communications team, following the charity's guidelines.

  3. Show up

    Direct service: helping set up wishes before beneficiaries arrive, joining wish trips, and taking photos for the families' memory albums.

  4. Volunteer at events

    Around twenty of us help run Ambulance Wish's charity golf day each September, plus community outreach events through the year.

03

How it started

Project Wish began with a stack of posters. In 2024, before there was any club, we put them up in cafés around Robertson Quay for Ambulance Wish Singapore's Cycle of Peace charity ride.

We started the club a year later to show other students that small, ordinary actions can change someone's day, and sometimes the last good day they have.

  1. 2024

    Cycle of Peace posters go up around Robertson Quay. First work with Ambulance Wish Singapore.

  2. Aug 2025

    Project Wish is founded at SAS, one of three new clubs approved out of 50+ applications.

  3. 2025–26

    The school's first social media fundraiser. First direct service trips with Ambulance Wish.

  4. 2026

    Recognised at the Honors assembly with the New Service Club Award from the SAS Eagle Service Council.

  5. Summer 2026

    A member interns at Ambulance Wish and builds automations that save its two-person team about three hours a week.

  6. 2026–27

    Storytelling for Ambulance Wish, AMBY blind bag sales, and more members on wish setups.
